## 2. Key findings

### Grain and join cardinality
- **orders** is unique on `order_id` (99,441 orders). `customer_id` is one-per-order; `customer_unique_id` identifies the person: 96,096 unique people, of whom 2,997 have more than one `customer_id` (repeat buyers). No orphan references in orders↔customers, items→orders/products/sellers.
- **order_items** (1 order → many items): 9,803 orders have >1 item (max 21). Joining orders to items yields 113,425 rows vs 99,441 orders. **775 orders have no items** (unavailable 603, canceled 164, created 5, invoiced 2, shipped 1).
- **Multi-seller orders:** 1,278 orders (1.3% of orders with items) involve more than one seller.
- **payments** (1 order → many rows): 2,961 orders have multiple payment rows; orders×payments gives 103,887 rows. 1 order has no payment; 9 payment rows have value 0. Payment total differs from items+freight by more than 1 BRL in 249 of 98,665 comparable orders (vouchers/instalment interest likely, not verified).
- **reviews** (1 order → 0..many): `review_id` is not unique and `order_id` is not unique; (`review_id`,`order_id`) is. 547 orders have multiple reviews and 768 have none; orders×reviews gives 99,992 rows. Only 40,977 reviews contain comment text.
- **geolocation is not a lookup table:** 1,000,163 rows for 19,015 ZIP prefixes; 261,831 rows are exact duplicates. 17,972 prefixes have several rows (median 29, max 1,146); 17,781 have more than one distinct coordinate pair, 8,556 have multiple city spellings and 8 span more than one state. A raw join of customers to geolocation yields 15,083,733 rows (vs 99,441 customers) — it must be aggregated to one row per prefix first. 42 coordinates fall outside a rough Brazil bounding box. 278 customer and 7 seller ZIP prefixes are absent from geolocation.
- **products:** 610 products lack a category (and name/description/photo fields); 2 categories have no English translation; 2 products miss weight/dimensions, 4 have zero weight.

### Order status and timestamps
- Status: delivered 96,478 (97.02%), shipped 1,107 (1.11%), canceled 625 (0.63%), unavailable 609 (0.61%), invoiced 314 (0.32%), processing 301 (0.3%), created 5 (0.01%), approved 2 (0.0%).
- Missing timestamps: approved 160, carrier handoff 1,783, customer delivery 2,965; purchase and estimated-delivery are 100% complete. No unparseable values.
- Status/timestamp inconsistencies: 8 `delivered` orders lack a delivery date; 6 non-delivered orders have one; 14 delivered orders lack an approval time.
- Invalid sequences: carrier date before purchase 166, carrier before approval 1,359, customer delivery before carrier handoff 23; approval/delivery before purchase and estimate before purchase: 0. 1,382 orders have at least one violation.
- Delivered orders with a delivery date: 96,470. Purchase→delivery days: median 10.22, mean 12.56, p95 29.27, p99 46.05, max 209.63; 306 exceed 60 days. Median promised lead time is 23.2 days. **8.11%** of delivered orders arrived after the estimate.
- Coverage: purchases span 2016-09 to 2018-10 (26 calendar months). Zero orders in 2016-11; fewer than 100 orders in 2016-09, 2016-12, 2018-09, 2018-10 (partial/ramp-up months). Peak month 2017-11 (7,544 orders).

### Geography and sellers
- Customers are concentrated: SP is 41.98% of orders and SP+RJ+MG 66.61%. 59.74% of sellers are in SP; sellers cover 23 states vs 27 for customers. 36.18% of item rows are same-state shipments.
- 2,970 sellers have delivered orders; 1,237 have ≥10, 627 have ≥30 and 210 have ≥100. The top 10% of sellers account for 67.82% of seller-order pairs.

### Reviews
- Score distribution: 1★ 11,424, 2★ 3,151, 3★ 8,179, 4★ 19,142, 5★ 57,328 (14.7% are 1–2★). 99.33% of delivered orders have a review.
- Timing anomalies: 5,127 reviews were created before the recorded delivery date, 64 before the purchase date, and 2,863 belong to non-delivered orders.
- Descriptive only (no causal claim): mean score is 4.29 for on-time vs 2.57 for late delivered orders.

## 3. Feasibility assessment

| Analysis | Feasible? | Basis | Main risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery performance (actual vs estimated, lead time) | **Yes** | 96,470 delivered orders with complete purchase/estimate/delivery timestamps | Right-censoring: undelivered orders excluded; 1,382 orders with inconsistent intermediate timestamps (use purchase→customer delivery only); long-tail outliers |
| Regional comparison (customer state) | **Yes** (state level); city/ZIP weak | All 27 customer states present; no orphan customers | Heavy SP/RJ/MG concentration → small samples in northern states; need minimum-N rules |
| Seller-level analysis | **Partly** | 627 sellers with ≥30 delivered orders | Long-tailed volume; 1,278 multi-seller orders make order-level delay/review ambiguous to attribute (delivery dates are order-level, not item/seller-level) |
| Customer review analysis | **Yes** (scores); text limited | 99,224 reviews | Self-selection (reviews are voluntary); multiple reviews per order; only 40,977 with text (Portuguese); timing anomalies; review reflects whole order, not one seller |
| Approximate distance (seller↔customer) | **Yes, approximate** | 99.51% of item rows have ZIP centroids for both parties | Centroid from a non-unique prefix table (median within-prefix coordinate SD ≈ 0.72 km, rough); straight-line not road distance; 5-digit prefixes only; multi-seller orders need item-level distance |
| Causal claims (e.g. lateness → low score) | **No** | Observational | Confounding (product, region, seller), selection into reviewing |

## 4. Limitations and bias risks
- **Selection/survivorship:** delivery metrics cover only delivered orders (96,478 of 99,441); canceled/unavailable orders (1,234) are invisible to delivery KPIs. Orders ending the window (2018-09/10) may be incompletely observed.
- **Attribution:** order-level timestamps and review scores cannot be assigned to a single seller when an order has several; recommend excluding or flagging multi-seller orders in seller scorecards.
- **Reviews:** voluntary and skewed positive; customers with bad experiences may be more (or less) likely to respond — direction is not identifiable from this data.
- **Window and representativeness:** one marketplace, 2016-09 to 2018-10, with an apparent ramp-up; company-wide conclusions should not be drawn; whether the data is a full or sampled population cannot be determined from the files.
- **Not verified:** payment/item gaps, causes of timestamp inversions, and whether late 2018 months are truncated by extraction date.

## 5. Recommended next steps
1. Build a cleaned order-grain fact table: aggregate payments and reviews to one row per order (latest review, flag duplicates) *before* joining; keep item-grain table separately for seller/product analysis.
2. Build a one-row-per-ZIP-prefix geolocation table (median lat/lng after de-duplication, drop out-of-bounds points) and compute haversine distance at item level.
3. Define rules for timestamp anomalies (flag, do not delete) and a minimum-sample threshold for region/seller comparisons.
4. Restrict trend analyses to complete months (exclude 2016-09, 2016-12, 2018-09, 2018-10 or mark them partial).
5. Then proceed to EDA, KPI definitions (on-time rate, lead time, review score) and modelling/dashboard scoping.
